PiLoop Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

1. About PiLoop

PiLoop is a multilingual platform designed to connect people, businesses and service providers within the Pi Network ecosystem.

2. Information

PiLoop may process information provided by users, including usernames, listings, descriptions and contact details. Information submitted in public listings may be visible to other users.

3. Pi Network Authentication

When Pi authentication is used, information authorized by the user may be processed to provide application features. Never share your wallet passphrase or private keys with PiLoop.

4. Use of Information

Information may be used to operate the platform, display listings, support users and improve application functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

PiLoop uses GitHub Pages for hosting and may interact with Pi Network services. These services have their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

Users should avoid publishing sensitive personal information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. User Rights

Users may request access, correction or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable law.

8. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the developer through the PiLoop GitHub repository.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as PiLoop develops.
