PiLoop Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

1. About PiLoop

PiLoop is an independent multilingual listing platform operated by Payman. It is not an official Pi Network service.

2. Information we process

We process Pi usernames and user identifiers, account and session information, listings, descriptions, optional location and contact details, uploaded photos, favorites, and payment identifiers, amounts, transaction identifiers, status and promotion dates. Hosting and backend services may process technical logs such as IP addresses.

3. Public information

Approved listings, including their photos, descriptions, location and contact details, may be visible to others. Do not publish sensitive information or information about others without permission.

4. Purposes

We use information to authenticate users, operate listings and favorites, moderate content, process and reconcile payments, prevent abuse, provide support and maintain security. Where applicable, processing relies on providing requested services, legitimate interests in security and operation, legal obligations, or consent where required.

5. Pi authentication and payments

Pi authentication and payment services are used to verify identity and transactions. Never provide your wallet passphrase or private keys to PiLoop. Testnet payments use Test-Pi, not real monetary value.

6. Service providers and transfers

GitHub Pages hosts the website; Supabase provides database, authentication, storage and backend functions; Pi Network provides authentication and payment services. These providers may process information in other countries. Applicable safeguards and provider terms govern such processing.

7. Storage and retention

Browser storage may retain language preferences, session information and pending payment references. Account and listing information is retained as needed to provide the service. Payment and security records may be retained for reconciliation, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Specific retention periods have not yet been established.

8. Security

Access controls and server-side payment verification help protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing, withdraw consent where applicable, and complain to a competent data protection authority. Contact us to submit a request. Some records may need to be retained for lawful purposes.

10. Contact and changes

For private requests email paymanpayman167@gmail.com. Do not post personal information in public GitHub issues. This policy may be updated as PiLoop develops.
